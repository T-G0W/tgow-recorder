## TGOW Recorder 0.1.0

The first release, for Windows 10 and 11 and for Linux.

- Fullscreen overlay on Alt+Z with Instant Replay, Record, Livestream, screenshots, settings and a gallery
- Instant replay in RAM or on disk, save the last 30 seconds, 1, 5 or 10 minutes or the whole buffer
- Recording with pause, chapter markers, automatic splitting and a countdown
- Livestreaming to Twitch, YouTube, Kick, Rumble, WHIP and custom RTMP or SRT servers
- Hardware encoding with NVENC, AMF, Quick Sync, VA-API and Vulkan Video; H264, HEVC, AV1, VP8 and VP9
- Several audio tracks, each mixing devices and single applications
- Game detection for Steam, Proton, Epic, GOG, Xbox and Wine games
- Keyboard and controller hotkeys, system tray, notifications and autostart
- English and German
